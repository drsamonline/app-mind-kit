What it does

Holds a worry until its window opens and will not solve it. Keeps a sleep diary and computes efficiency from bedtime, get-up time and the minutes you were awake.

What it refuses

No schedule, no restriction protocol, no recommended bedtime, and no verdict on a night. An unreadable store is never emptied — an empty list reads as “there is nothing to worry about”.
